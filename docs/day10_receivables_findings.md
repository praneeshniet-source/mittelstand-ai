
# Day 10 — Customer Payment & Receivables Analytics

## Objective
Analyse customer invoices, collections, outstanding receivables and overdue payments.

## Dataset
- Total invoices: 10
- Paid invoices: 4
- Unpaid invoices: 6
- Total invoiced: €28,600

## Key Financial Metrics
- Amount collected: €8,600
- Outstanding receivables: €20,000
- Overdue invoices: 2
- Overdue amount: €7,400
- Average payment delay: -3 days

## Customer Exposure
- Beta Manufacturing AG: €11,100
- Alpha Retail GmbH: €6,800
- Delta Trading BV: €2,100

## Business Insights
- Beta Manufacturing AG has the highest outstanding exposure.
- €7,400 in customer invoices is overdue.
- Paid invoices were settled an average of 3 days before their due dates.

## Technical Skills
- Python
- Pandas
- Data filtering and aggregation
- Date calculations
- Receivables analysis
- Customer-level financial reporting

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic and intended for demonstration. Payment delay is calculated using only paid invoices. The analysis uses 27 October 2026 as its reporting date.
