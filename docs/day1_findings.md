
# Mittelstand AI — Day 1 Business Findings

## 1. Business objective
Analyze invoice data to understand spending, payment status,
overdue invoices, and supplier-level expenditure.

## 2. Dataset overview
- Total invoices: 10
- Total invoice value: €16,100
- Reporting date: 27 October 2026
- Data source: Fictional synthetic invoice dataset

## 3. Key findings

### Finding 1: Overdue invoices
- Overdue unpaid invoices: 3
- Total overdue invoice value: €2,600
- Recommended action: Review the invoices and prioritize
  appropriate follow-up.

### Finding 2: Supplier spending
- Highest-spend supplier: Munich Tools AG
- Total invoice value: €8,200
- Recommended action: Investigate supplier spending patterns
  and compare them with purchasing needs.

### Finding 3: Payment status
- Paid invoice value: €7,400
- Unpaid invoice value: €8,700
- Recommended action: Review unpaid invoices and verify
  their current payment status.

## 4. Limitations
- The dataset is fictional and contains only 10 invoices.
- Invoice status is simplified.
- Partial payments are not represented.
- Overdue values are based on the invoice amount and status,
  not a separately calculated remaining balance.
- Findings should not be generalized to a real organization.

## 5. Next steps
- Learn SQL to answer business questions from structured data.
- Add data-quality checks.
- Add payment records to calculate outstanding balances.
- Build a dashboard after the metrics are verified.
